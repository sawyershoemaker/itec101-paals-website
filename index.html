Palmetto Animal Assisted Life Services

More independence, one dog at a time.

PAALS is a nonprofit in Columbia, South Carolina, that trains assistance dogs for people with disabilities. It also offers educational and recreational activities that help people learn and connect through working with dogs.

Explore Our Services

What we offer

  • Assistance dogs: Training that helps dogs support people with daily tasks.
  • Reading PAALS: Reading practice with a calm dog.
  • School PAALS: Activities that teach students about assistance dogs and teamwork.

Our mission

Help children and adults with disabilities live more independently through trained dogs and animal-assisted activities.
